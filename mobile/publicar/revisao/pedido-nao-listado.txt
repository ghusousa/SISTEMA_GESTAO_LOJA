App name: Gestão de Loja
Bundle ID: com.gestaoloja.app
Apple ID: o número em App Store Connect > o app > App Information > General Information > Apple ID

Describe your app and its intended audience:
Point of sale and stock control for small physical shops in Brazil (clothing, shoes, auto parts). Each shop runs the system on its own server, and the shop owner creates the accounts for the staff; there is no public sign-up. The app is used only by the owners and staff of the shops that run the system: barcode sales at the counter, stock by size and color, returns, cash count and reports.

Why should the app be unlisted?
The app is of no use to the general public: it only works with a shop's server and an account created by the shop's owner. App Review found it intended for a limited audience (Guideline 3.2) and pointed us to unlisted distribution. Unlisted, the staff of these shops can install it from a direct link without the app appearing in App Store search, charts or categories.

How will users get the link to the app?
We send the App Store link to each shop when its server is set up, and the owner shares it with the staff, together with the server address they type in the app.
