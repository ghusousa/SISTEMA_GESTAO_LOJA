Guideline 2.3.8 - App icon
The placeholder icon was replaced with the final one: a shop price tag with a barcode, in the app's colors. The App Store icon, the Home Screen icon and the launch screen all come from the same artwork, so they match at every size.

Guideline 2.1(a) - Demo QR code and images
Codes to scan are on this page (open it on a computer screen and point the app's camera at it):
https://loja-tria.vercel.app/codigos-para-teste
- Product barcodes (EAN-13) of the demo shop, to scan in "Vender" (point of sale) and in Entrada, Inventário, Devolução and Condicional.
- The QR code for "Leitor do computador" (the phone as the computer's barcode reader) is created live by the shop's computer and is valid for 5 minutes, so it cannot be a fixed image. The page has the steps: sign in to the demo shop in a computer browser, click "Celular de outra pessoa? Ler o QR", then in the app Mais > Leitor do computador > "Ler o QR do computador".
The same link is in the App Review notes, together with the demo server and the demo account.

Guideline 3.2 - Business
The app is for a limited audience: the owners and staff of the small shops that run this system on their own server. We have requested Unlisted App Distribution for it. Answers to your questions:
1. It is not restricted to a single company. Each shop that uses it is an independent small business, and inside a shop only its owner and staff use the app.
2. Yes, it is designed for a specific group: small physical shops in Brazil (clothing, shoes, auto parts) that run the system on their own server. A shop starts using it when its server is set up, at the shop's request.
3. None. Every feature is for the shop's staff at the counter: sales, stock, returns, cash count and reports.
4. The owner's account is created when the shop's server is set up; the owner then creates the staff accounts in the app (Mais > Usuários). There is no public sign-up.
5. No. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid features, and no one pays to open an account. The shop's customers pay for goods at the counter, outside the app.
