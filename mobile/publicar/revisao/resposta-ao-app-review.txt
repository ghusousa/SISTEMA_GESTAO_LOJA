Guideline 3.2 - Business
The app is for a general public audience: any small shop can download it and use it, without an invitation from us or any affiliation. The server it connects to is free and open source (MIT license): https://github.com/ghusousa/SISTEMA_GESTAO_LOJA. Answers to your questions:
1. No. Any shop can use the app. Each shop runs its own free copy of the server, and inside a shop its owner and staff use the app.
2. No, it is not limited to specific companies. Any company can use it on its own: the owner installs the free server on a computer of the shop with one command, following the public guide (the "Ainda não tem o sistema na loja?" link on the app's first screen opens it). No contract or approval from us is needed.
3. All of the app's features: point of sale with the camera as barcode reader, stock, returns, cash count and reports. Anyone who installs the free server uses all of them.
4. The installer of the free server creates the owner's account; the owner then creates the staff accounts in the app (Mais > Usuários). No invitation or pre-approved registration from us.
5. No. The app and the server are free, with no in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid features. The shop's customers pay for goods at the counter, outside the app.
