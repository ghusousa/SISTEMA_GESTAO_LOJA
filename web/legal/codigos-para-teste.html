Codes for testing the camera

Show this page on a computer screen (or print it) and point the app's camera at it. The products below exist in the demo shop, with fictitious data only.

1. Product barcodes (EAN-13)

In the app, tap “Ler código de barras” in the Vender tab (point of sale) and point the camera at a barcode: the item goes into the sale. The same barcodes work in Entrada, Inventário, Devolução and Condicional.

2. QR code: the phone as the computer's barcode reader

This QR code is created live by the shop's computer and is valid for 5 minutes, so it cannot be a fixed image. To get one:

  1. On a computer, open the demo shop in the browser and sign in with the demo account from the App Review notes.
  2. In Vender, click “Celular de outra pessoa? Ler o QR”. The QR code appears on the screen.
  3. In the app: Mais › Leitor do computador › “Ler o QR do computador”, and point the camera at the QR code.
  4. Now scan a barcode from section 1 with the phone: the item appears in the computer's sale.

Without a QR code: with the app open on the phone (same account), click “Abrir no app o leitor de código” on the computer. The app shows “O computador está chamando” with “Abrir o leitor”.
